This Privacy Policy explains how ripri ("ripri", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the ripri mobile application (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

ripri is strictly for adults (18 years or older). We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Priyadarshi Kumar ("the Operator"), based in Mumbai, India. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at mavericknew01@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Phone number — used to create and secure your account through SMS verification.
  • Profile details — such as your name or display name, age, bio, preferences, and photos you upload.
  • Identity verification — if you choose to get verified, a government ID image and a selfie. These are handled with restricted, private access (see Section 6).
  • Messages and content — chats, images, voice notes, and other content you send within the App.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location — used to show and sort potential matches near you. We use approximate (not precise GPS) location.
  • Device and usage data — such as device identifiers, push notification tokens, app interactions, and log data used to operate and improve the App.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, verify your phone number, and keep you signed in.
  • To show your profile to other users and help you discover and match with people.
  • To deliver messages, notifications, and app features.
  • To operate optional AI features (see Section 4).
  • To keep the community safe — including reviewing verification submissions and reports, detecting abuse, and enforcing our Community Guidelines and Terms.
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud and misuse.

4. AI features

The App may offer optional AI-assisted features (for example, conversation help or suggestions). When you use such a feature, the relevant content you submit may be processed by a third-party AI service (such as Google's Gemini) to generate a response. We do not use these features to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Do not share sensitive personal information in AI features.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Other users — your profile, photos, and the content you choose to share are visible to other users as part of the App's normal functioning.
  • Service providers — we use trusted providers to run the App, including Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications, hosting of app services) and Cloudinary (media storage and delivery). They process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
  • Legal reasons — where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or the Operator.

6. Media, verification, and retention

  • Profile photos and chat media are stored with our media provider and delivered within the App.
  • Some media may be sent as "view-once"; we apply reasonable measures for such content, but no delivery method can be guaranteed against a determined recipient (for example, screenshots).
  • Verification images (ID and selfie) are uploaded using private, authenticated delivery so they are not publicly accessible, and are used only to confirm your identity and safety of the community.
  • We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we remove your data as described in Section 8.

7. Security

We use technical measures to protect your data, including restricted database rules, signed and access-controlled media uploads, private delivery for verification images, and app-integrity checks. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit most profile information within the App.
  • Account deletion — you can permanently delete your account from within the App (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and associated personal data, and deletes your account credentials. Some records may be retained where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, reports may be kept in anonymised form).
  • Notifications and location — you can control push notifications and location permissions through your device settings.

To make a request, contact mavericknew01@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how ripri ("ripri", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the ripri mobile application (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

The App is operated by Priyadarshi Kumar ("the Operator"), based in Mumbai, India. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at mavericknew01@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Phone number — used to create and secure your account through SMS verification.
  • Email and password — if you choose to register or sign in with email.
  • Profile details — such as your name or display name, age, bio, gender, interests, lifestyle preferences, relationship intent, and photos you upload.
  • Identity verification — if you choose to get verified, a government ID image and a selfie. These are handled with restricted, private access (see Section 6).
  • Messages and content — chats, images, voice notes, GIFs, stickers, and other content you send within the App.
  • Emergency contacts — if you use Safety Mode, you may provide up to 3 emergency contact names and phone numbers. You confirm you have permission from these contacts to share their information with us for safety alert purposes.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location — used to show and sort potential matches near you. We use approximate (not precise GPS) location, rounded to approximately 1 km before storage, for discovery purposes.
  • Safety Mode location — if you activate Safety Mode, your location is tracked in the foreground (with your explicit consent) and shared with your pre-set emergency contacts. This location is also rounded to approximately 1 km before storage.
  • Device and usage data — such as device identifiers, push notification tokens, app interactions, and log data used to operate and improve the App.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, verify your phone number, and keep you signed in.
  • To show your profile to other users and help you discover and match with people.
  • To deliver messages, notifications, and app features.
  • To operate optional AI features (see Section 4).
  • To enable audio and video calling between matched users (see Section 5).
  • To enable Safety Mode and SOS alerts for your personal safety (see Section 6).
  • To verify your identity using facial recognition technology (see Section 6).
  • To keep the community safe — including reviewing verification submissions and reports, detecting abuse, and enforcing our Community Guidelines and Terms.
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud and misuse.

4. AI features

The App may offer optional AI-assisted features (for example, profile suggestions, conversation help, or bio writing assistance). When you use such a feature, the relevant content you submit may be processed by a third-party AI service (Google's Gemini) to generate a response. The processing occurs server-side; your Gemini API key is not stored on your device.

We do not use these features to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Do not share sensitive personal information in AI features.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Other users — your profile, photos, and the content you choose to share are visible to other users as part of the App's normal functioning.
  • Service providers — we use trusted providers to run the App. These include:
    • Google Firebase — authentication, database, cloud functions, notifications, and app infrastructure.
    • Cloudinary — media storage and delivery for profile photos, chat media, and verification images.
    • Agora — real-time audio and video calling infrastructure. When you make or receive a call, audio/video streams are routed through Agora's servers. No call content is stored by Agora after the call ends.
    • AWS Rekognition (Amazon Web Services) — facial recognition for identity verification. Your ID photo and selfie are sent to AWS Rekognition to compare faces and confirm your identity. Images are processed in real time and are not stored by AWS after comparison.
    • GIPHY — GIF and sticker search within the chat. Your search queries are sent to GIPHY's API to retrieve results. GIPHY may collect usage data per their own privacy policy.
    • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery.
    These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
  • Emergency contacts — if you activate Safety Mode and trigger an SOS alert, your approximate location and the name/photo of the person you are meeting are shared with your pre-set emergency contacts via SMS.
  • Legal reasons — where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or the Operator.

6. Media, verification, biometric processing, and retention

  • Profile photos and chat media are stored with our media provider (Cloudinary) and delivered within the App.
  • Some media may be sent as "view-once"; we apply reasonable measures for such content, but no delivery method can be guaranteed against a determined recipient (for example, screenshots). View-once media may be retained temporarily for safety and moderation review.
  • Verification images (ID and selfie) are uploaded using private, authenticated delivery so they are not publicly accessible, and are used only to confirm your identity and safety of the community.
  • Biometric processing: When you submit your ID and selfie for verification, we use AWS Rekognition (a facial recognition service by Amazon Web Services) to compare the face in your selfie with the face on your ID document. This constitutes biometric data processing. You are asked for explicit consent before this processing occurs. The comparison happens in real time; we store only the match result (verified/not verified) and a similarity score, not biometric templates.
  • We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we remove your data as described in Section 8.

7. Security

We use technical measures to protect your data, including restricted database rules, signed and access-controlled media uploads, private delivery for verification images, server-side secret management, rate limiting on API endpoints, code obfuscation, app-integrity checks (Play Integrity), and screenshot blocking on sensitive screens. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit most profile information within the App.
  • Account deletion — you can permanently delete your account from within the App (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and associated personal data, and deletes your account credentials. Some records may be retained where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, reports may be kept in anonymised form).
  • Notifications and location — you can control push notifications and location permissions through your device settings.
  • Withdraw biometric consent — you may choose not to verify your identity. Verification is optional and not required to use the App's core features.

To make a request, contact mavericknew01@gmail.com.

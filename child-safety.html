Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 25 August 2026

ripri is an adults-only (18+) dating and social application. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we uphold and the steps we take to keep children off our platform and to prevent, detect, and respond to any such content or behaviour.

1. Adults only — no minors

  • ripri is intended solely for users aged 18 years or older.
  • Users confirm they are 18+ during sign-up, and our Terms of Service prohibit anyone under 18 from creating an account or using the app.
  • If we become aware that a user is under 18, we remove the account and delete associated data.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

  • We strictly prohibit any content, conduct, solicitation, or communication that sexualises, exploits, endangers, or abuses minors.
  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, or attempting to distribute CSAM is forbidden and will result in immediate account termination.
  • Grooming, enticement, sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to make contact with a minor for sexual purposes is forbidden.

3. In-app reporting and blocking

  • Every user can report a profile, message, or piece of content directly inside the app.
  • Every user can block another user at any time.
  • Reports of suspected child endangerment or CSAM are treated as the highest priority.

4. How we respond

  • We review reports and take action, including content removal and account termination.
  • We remove confirmed CSAM and preserve relevant information as required by law.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material and related offences to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant Indian law-enforcement and statutory authorities, as required.

5. Legal compliance

ripri complies with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions in which it operates, including India, and cooperates with law-enforcement and child-protection authorities in the investigation of child safety matters.

To delete your ripri account and all associated data, open the ripri app, go to Settings, and tap ‘Delete Account’. Your profile, photos, messages, and all personal data will be permanently deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

This action cannot be undone. Once you delete your account, your data is permanently removed.

What gets deleted

  • Profile information (name, date of birth, bio, preferences)
  • Photos
  • Messages and chat history
  • Matches and likes
  • Location data
  • Any other personal data

What is retained

No personal data is retained after deletion. Anonymous, aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you may be kept.

ripri is a space to meet people respectfully. These guidelines keep the community safe. Breaking them may lead to content removal, warnings, or account termination. By using ripri, you agree to follow them.

  1. Be 18 or older. ripri is only for adults. Never interact with, or present yourself as, a minor.
  2. Be real. Use your own photos and honest information. No impersonation, fake profiles, or misleading identities.
  3. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, bullying, threats, or discrimination based on religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, ethnicity, disability, or any other identity.
  4. Get consent. Respect boundaries. Do not send sexual or explicit content to anyone who has not clearly welcomed it.
  5. No nudity or sexual exploitation. Do not post pornographic content, and never share content that sexualises minors — this is illegal and results in an immediate permanent ban and reporting to authorities.
  6. No violence or illegal activity. Do not threaten or promote violence, self-harm, or any unlawful acts, or use ripri to buy, sell, or arrange anything illegal.
  7. No scams or spam. Do not ask users for money, promote schemes, advertise, solicit, or send repetitive/unwanted messages.
  8. Protect privacy. Do not share other people's private information, and do not screenshot, record, or redistribute someone's photos, messages, or "view-once" content.
  9. Keep it safe offline. Meet in public places, tell someone you trust, and never send money to someone you haven't met. Your safety comes first.
  10. Report and block. If someone breaks these rules or makes you uncomfortable, use the in-app block and report tools. We review reports and act on them.
  11. Play fair. Do not use bots, automation, or tricks to bypass verification, safety systems, or app integrity, and do not create new accounts to evade a ban.
Serious violations — especially anything involving minors, non-consensual intimate content, or credible threats — lead to immediate removal and may be reported to law enforcement.
